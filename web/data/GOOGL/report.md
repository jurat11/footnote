# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
Alphabet Inc. operates in Services-Computer Programming, Data Processing, Etc.. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $402.8B [1] and net income of $132.2B [2]. That is a net margin of 32.8% [3]. Revenue rose versus FY2024 (15.1% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 59.7% [5] in FY2025; it stayed broadly flat over the period. Operating margin stood at 32.0% [6]. Net margin was 32.8% [3]. Return on equity was 31.8% [7] and return on assets 22.2% [8].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 15.1% [4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $257.6B [9] in FY2021 to $402.8B [1] in FY2025.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 2.01x [10], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.12x [11]; leverage trended higher over the period. Total assets were $595.3B [12] against stockholders' equity of $415.3B [13].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $164.7B [14] in FY2025. After capital expenditures of $91.4B [15], free cash flow was $73.3B [16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 18.2% [17]. Operating income covered interest expense 175.32x [18] over.

## What the data cannot tell you
These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
