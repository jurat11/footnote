# JOHNSON & JOHNSON (JNJ) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
JOHNSON & JOHNSON operates in Pharmaceutical Preparations. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $94.2B [1] and net income of $26.8B [2]. That is a net margin of 28.5% [3]. Revenue rose versus FY2024 (6.0% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 67.9% [5] in FY2025; it stayed broadly flat over the period. Net margin was 28.5% [3]. Return on equity was 32.9% [6] and return on assets 13.5% [7].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 6.0% [4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $82.6B [8] in FY2021 to $94.2B [1] in FY2025.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 1.03x [9], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.51x [10]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $199.2B [11] against stockholders' equity of $81.5B [12].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $24.5B [13] in FY2025. After capital expenditures of $4.8B [14], free cash flow was $19.7B [15]. That is a free cash flow margin of 20.9% [16]. Interest coverage was not available (missing input(s): operating_income.).
