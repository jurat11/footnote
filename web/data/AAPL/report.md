# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
Apple Inc. operates in Electronic Computers. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $416.2B [1] and net income of $112.0B [2]. That is a net margin of 26.9% [3]. Revenue rose versus FY2024 (6.4% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 46.9% [5] in FY2025; it trended higher over the period. Operating margin stood at 32.0% [6]. Net margin was 26.9% [3]. Return on equity was 151.9% [7] and return on assets 31.2% [8].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 6.4% [4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $365.8B [9] in FY2021 to $416.2B [1] in FY2025.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 0.89x [10], meaning current liabilities exceed current assets. Debt to equity was 1.23x [11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $359.2B [12] against stockholders' equity of $73.7B [13].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $111.5B [14] in FY2025. After capital expenditures of $12.7B [15], free cash flow was $98.8B [16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 23.7% [17]. Interest coverage was not available (missing input(s): interest_expense.).
