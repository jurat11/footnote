NVIDIA CORP (NVDA) — SEC filing analysis

Summary

NVIDIA CORP operates in Semiconductors & Related Devices. In FY2026 it reported revenue of $215.9B[1] and net income of $120.1B[2]. That is a net margin of 55.6%[3]. Revenue rose versus FY2025 (65.5%[4]).

Profitability

Gross margin was 71.1%[5] in FY2026; it trended higher over the period. Operating margin stood at 60.4%[6]. Net margin was 55.6%[3]. Return on equity was 76.3%[7] and return on assets 58.1%[8].

Growth

Year-over-year revenue growth was 65.5%[4] in FY2026. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $26.9B[9] in FY2022 to $215.9B[1] in FY2026.

Balance sheet strength

The current ratio was 3.91x[10], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.05x[11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $206.8B[12] against stockholders' equity of $157.3B[13].

Cash generation

Cash from operations was $102.7B[14] in FY2026. After capital expenditures of $6.0B[15], free cash flow was $96.7B[16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 44.8%[17]. Operating income covered interest expense 503.42x[18] over.

What the data cannot tell you

These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
