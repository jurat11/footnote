# COCA COLA CO (KO) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
COCA COLA CO operates in Beverages. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $47.9B [1] and net income of $13.1B [2]. That is a net margin of 27.3% [3]. Revenue was little changed versus FY2024 (1.9% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 61.6% [5] in FY2025; it stayed broadly flat over the period. Operating margin stood at 28.7% [6]. Net margin was 27.3% [3]. Return on equity was 40.7% [7] and return on assets 12.5% [8].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 1.9% [4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $38.7B [9] in FY2021 to $47.9B [1] in FY2025.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 1.46x [10], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 1.31x [11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $104.8B [12] against stockholders' equity of $32.2B [13].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $7.4B [14] in FY2025. After capital expenditures of $2.1B [15], free cash flow was $5.3B [16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 11.0% [17]. Operating income covered interest expense 8.32x [18] over.

## What the data cannot tell you
Some metrics were not separately tagged in this company's XBRL filings and are omitted rather than estimated: current debt, total liabilities. These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
