# NIKE, Inc. (NKE) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
NIKE, Inc. operates in Rubber & Plastics Footwear. In FY2026 it reported revenue of $46.4B [1] and net income of $3.1B [2]. That is a net margin of 6.7% [3]. Revenue was little changed versus FY2025 (0.2% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 42.9% [5] in FY2026; it trended lower over the period. Net margin was 6.7% [3]. Return on equity was 20.9% [6] and return on assets 8.1% [7].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 0.2% [4] in FY2026. Across the reported years, revenue stayed broadly flat over the period. Revenue moved from $46.7B [8] in FY2022 to $46.4B [1] in FY2026.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 1.96x [9], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.53x [10]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $38.4B [11] against stockholders' equity of $14.9B [12].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $2.9B [13] in FY2026. After capital expenditures of $684.0M [14], free cash flow was $2.2B [15]. That is a free cash flow margin of 4.7% [16]. Interest coverage was not available (missing input(s): operating_income, interest_expense.).
