Walmart Inc. (WMT) — SEC filing analysis

Summary

Walmart Inc. operates in Retail-Variety Stores. In FY2026 it reported revenue of $706.4B[1] and net income of $21.9B[2]. That is a net margin of 3.1%[3]. Revenue rose versus FY2025 (4.7%[4]).

Profitability

Gross margin was 24.2%[5] in FY2026; it stayed broadly flat over the period. Operating margin stood at 4.2%[6]. Net margin was 3.1%[3]. Return on equity was 22.0%[7] and return on assets 7.7%[8].

Growth

Year-over-year revenue growth was 4.7%[4] in FY2026. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $567.8B[9] in FY2022 to $706.4B[1] in FY2026.

Balance sheet strength

The current ratio was 0.79x[10], meaning current liabilities exceed current assets. Debt to equity was 0.38x[11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $284.7B[12] against stockholders' equity of $99.6B[13].

Cash generation

Cash from operations was $41.6B[14] in FY2026. After capital expenditures of $26.6B[15], free cash flow was $14.9B[16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 2.1%[17]. Interest coverage was not available (missing input(s): interest_expense.).

What the data cannot tell you

Some metrics were not separately tagged in this company's XBRL filings and are omitted rather than estimated: interest expense, total liabilities. These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
