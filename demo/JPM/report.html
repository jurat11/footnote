JPMORGAN CHASE & CO (JPM) — SEC filing analysis

Summary

JPMORGAN CHASE & CO operates in National Commercial Banks. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $182.4B[1] and net income of $57.0B[2]. That is a net margin of 31.3%[3]. Revenue rose versus FY2024 (2.8%[4]).

Profitability

The company reports no cost of revenue (typical of banks and insurers), so gross margin is not applicable. Net margin was 31.3%[3]. Return on equity was 15.7%[5] and return on assets 1.3%[6].

Growth

Year-over-year revenue growth was 2.8%[4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $121.6B[7] in FY2021 to $182.4B[1] in FY2025.

Balance sheet strength

Debt to equity was 0.18x[8]; leverage stayed broadly flat over the period. Total assets were $4.4T[9] against stockholders' equity of $362.4B[10].

Cash generation

Cash from operations was -$147.8B[11] in FY2025. Interest coverage was not available (missing input(s): operating_income, interest_expense.).

What the data cannot tell you

Some metrics were not separately tagged in this company's XBRL filings and are omitted rather than estimated: capex, cost of revenue, current assets, current liabilities, interest expense, long term debt, operating income. As a financial-sector filer it reports no cost of revenue, so the gross-margin-based view is unavailable. These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
