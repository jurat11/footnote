# MICROSOFT CORP (MSFT) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
MICROSOFT CORP operates in Services-Prepackaged Software. In FY2026 it reported revenue of $331.8B [1] and net income of $133.7B [2]. That is a net margin of 40.3% [3]. Revenue rose versus FY2025 (17.8% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 67.9% [5] in FY2026; it stayed broadly flat over the period. Operating margin stood at 46.8% [6]. Net margin was 40.3% [3]. Return on equity was 30.2% [7] and return on assets 17.6% [8].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 17.8% [4] in FY2026. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $198.3B [9] in FY2022 to $331.8B [1] in FY2026.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 1.23x [10], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.09x [11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $758.4B [12] against stockholders' equity of $442.4B [13].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $182.9B [14] in FY2026. After capital expenditures of $115.9B [15], free cash flow was $67.0B [16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 20.2% [17]. Operating income covered interest expense 50.88x [18] over.

## What the data cannot tell you
These filings also cannot speak to forward guidance, segment-level economics, unit volumes, pricing power, competitive position, or management quality. They are a backward-looking, GAAP view of what was reported, and every figure above is cited to the exact 10-K it came from.
