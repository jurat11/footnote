# AMAZON COM INC (AMZN) — SEC filing analysis

## Summary
AMAZON COM INC operates in Retail-Catalog & Mail-Order Houses. In FY2025 it reported revenue of $716.9B [1] and net income of $77.7B [2]. That is a net margin of 10.8% [3]. Revenue rose versus FY2024 (12.4% [4]).

## Profitability
Gross margin was 50.3% [5] in FY2025; it trended higher over the period. Operating margin stood at 11.2% [6]. Net margin was 10.8% [3]. Return on equity was 18.9% [7] and return on assets 9.5% [8].

## Growth
Year-over-year revenue growth was 12.4% [4] in FY2025. Across the reported years, revenue trended higher over the period. Revenue moved from $469.8B [9] in FY2021 to $716.9B [1] in FY2025.

## Balance sheet strength
The current ratio was 1.05x [10], meaning current assets exceed current liabilities. Debt to equity was 0.17x [11]; leverage trended lower over the period. Total assets were $818.0B [12] against stockholders' equity of $411.1B [13].

## Cash generation
Cash from operations was $139.5B [14] in FY2025. After capital expenditures of $131.8B [15], free cash flow was $7.7B [16]. That is a free cash flow margin of 1.1% [17]. Operating income covered interest expense 35.17x [18] over.
